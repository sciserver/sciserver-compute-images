# SIXTE in SciServer – Quick Start Guide

***

You are now inside a Jupyterlab environment pre-configured with:

    * HEASoft (v6.36)
    * SIXTE (v3.5.0) and SIMPUT (v2.8.0)
    * SIXTE instrument files for:
        * Athena WFI (v1.11.4) & X-IFU (05-2026)
        * XRISM (v1.2.0)
        * SRG (v1.9.2)
        * AXIS (v3.1.1)

***

## Key Folder Paths

* SIXTE source folder: /opt/sixte-src
* SIMPUT source installation: /opt/simput-src/
* SIXTE build: /opt/sixte/sixte
* SIMPUT build: /opt/sixte/simput
* Instrument files:
* SIXTE Manual: sixte_cookbooks/simulator_manual.pdf
